WELCOME TO THE URMI

A Comfortable Base for Your Haridwar Journey

The Urmi is a boutique hotel in Haridwar, located near Shantikunj on Rishikesh Road. We bring together contemporary comfort and the warmth of traditional Indian hospitality.

Our approach is simple: provide clean and comfortable accommodation, thoughtful service, wholesome vegetarian dining and a welcoming environment for every guest.

Whether your visit is spiritual, family-oriented, leisure focused or connected with a wider journey through Uttarakhand, The Urmi offers a peaceful and convenient place to stay.
